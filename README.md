## 📸 About The Project

**Shri Computers & Multi Services** is a digital service center website designed to make online government and computer-related services easier for customers.

The website provides information about:

- Government document services
- Online applications
- Xerox and printing
- Scanning
- PAN and Aadhaar services
- Revenue services
- Education and scholarship forms
- Job applications
- Business registrations
- Lamination and PVC cards
- Typing and documentation
- Required documents
- Service prices
- Shop location

# 📋 Government Documents & Required Documents

Every government service should have a dedicated information section.

When a customer selects a service, show:

### 1. Service Name

Example:

**उत्पन्न दाखला / Income Certificate**

### 2. Service Price

Example:

**Service Charge: ₹XXX**

### 3. Required Documents

Example:

- Aadhaar Card
- Mobile Number
- Address Proof
- Ration Card
- Income-related documents
- Passport-size photograph
- Applicant information
- Additional documents if required

### 4. Why Is It Required?

Explain why the certificate/service is commonly used.

Example:

**Income Certificate can be required for:**

- Scholarship
- Education
- Government schemes
- Fee concessions
- Government benefits

### 5. Application Information

Show:

- Where to apply
- Basic procedure
- Expected information
- Important instructions

> ⚠️ Document requirements and government fees can change. Users should verify the latest requirements before submitting an application.
